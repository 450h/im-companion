**MED 222 IM Companion v0.5.0: complete course content (midterm + final)**

**Install or update:** download `MED-222-IM-Companion-Setup-0.5.0.exe` below and run it. If you already have v0.1.0, install over it and your progress is kept. If Windows shows *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info → Run anyway** (the app is free and not code-signed; checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`).

**New in this version:**
- **All 90 blueprint topics are now available**: the 73 midterm topics across all 10 systems, plus the 17 final-only topics (COVID-19, viral infections, FUO, malaria, meningo-encephalitis, kidney in systemic disease, shock, bronchiectasis/abscess, pleural effusion, ILD, vasculitis, prolactinoma, TTP, lymphomas, DLBCL, tumour lysis, febrile neutropenia).
- **Chapter exams for every system** and fuller **mock midterm (EMQ) and mock final (MCQ)** papers.
- **The Final plan:** from the midterm date (1 Dec), the Plan page offers to create your Final plan. It adds the final-only topics as new study and a revision pass for everything you studied for the midterm, with fresh spaced reviews.
- **EMQ Volumes 1–3:** every blueprint-relevant item now links to the section to study when you get it wrong.

Every topic was written from the cited pages by two independent scanners plus a consolidator, then verified against its cited pages by a separate checker before release.
