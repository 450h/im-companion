**MED 222 IM Companion v0.1.0: first release**

**Install:**
1. Download `MED-222-IM-Companion-Setup-0.1.0.exe` below.
2. If Windows shows *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info → Run anyway**. The app is free and not code-signed. You can check the file against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

**What's inside:**
- **Midterm planner** (exam Tue 1 Dec), built from the Med 611 blueprint and the class Excel plan: Learn → Consolidate → Final revision, with Sun–Thu study days. Progress logs itself as you read and answer.
- **Content for Gastroenterology, Infectious Diseases and Nephrology** (23 topics). Every point cites the book and page. Each topic has section quizzes, a topic quiz, worked examples, flashcards and practice questions in the EMQ style. Every topic was checked against its cited pages before release.
- **Chapter exams** for GI, ID and Nephrology, plus **mock midterm and mock final papers** (they grow with each update).
- **EMQ Volumes answer sheet**: practise *EMQs for Medical Students* Vols 1–3 with your book open, and get marked with explanations.
- **OSPE practice** with openly licensed ECG, X-ray, blood-film and clinical images.
- Spaced reviews (1/3/7/14 days), a weak-areas drill, the Progress dashboard, backups and export.

The other systems show **⏳ Content pending** until their update arrives. You can study them from the book and tap "Studied from the book" to keep your plan on track.

*For exam study only. Not clinical guidance.*
